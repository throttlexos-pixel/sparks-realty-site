Our Story

We got tired of watching great agents get a bad deal.

The traditional brokerage model is broken. Agents do 95% of the work — prospecting, marketing, showing, negotiating, closing — and then hand over 30%, 40%, sometimes 50% of their commission to a brokerage that provides a logo and a desk.

Sparks Realty was founded to fix that equation. We built a lean, technology-forward brokerage that gives agents the economics they deserve: an 80/20 split, a $15,000 annual cap, and AI-powered tools that actually help you close more business.

Located in the heart of Sparks, Nevada, we serve agents across the greater Reno-Sparks market. Our approach is simple — provide the best agent economics in Northern Nevada, back it with modern technology, and maintain the kind of direct, personal broker access that corporate franchises can't offer.

Our Mission

“To create the most agent-friendly brokerage in Northern Nevada — where better economics and better tools drive better outcomes.”
